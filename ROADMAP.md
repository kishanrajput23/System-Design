## 🚀 Goal

By completing this roadmap, I aim to:

* Build a strong understanding of scalable system architecture.
* Design reliable, fault-tolerant, and highly available systems.
* Prepare for System Design interviews.
* Develop the ability to analyze real-world architectures and make informed design trade-offs.
* Maintain a long-term System Design knowledge base for continuous learning and reference.
